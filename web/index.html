    ⚕️ Medical disclaimer

    CardiaNova is a software demonstration, not a medical device and not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Symptom interpretation uses hand-written rule-based patterns, and the risk percentage comes from a statistical model trained on a single public dataset that has not been validated on any local population. Both can be wrong. Never delay seeking care because of something you read here — in a suspected emergency call 108 (India), 112 or 911 immediately.
